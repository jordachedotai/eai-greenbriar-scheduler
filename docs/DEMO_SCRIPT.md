# Demo script v2: four minutes in the room

Presenter: Jordache. Devrin frames. Mock mode on. State loaded: `council`, which is eighteen portfolio companies staggered across four assistants, with AIT Worldwide Logistics (Peggy's) not started.

## Beat 0: login and the morning view (20 seconds)
Sign in as Peggy. Rows view, "My companies." Work strip reads: Waiting on you 1, Waiting on others 3, Not started 1, Confirmed 4 of 20. "This is what Peggy sees at eight in the morning. One thing needs her. Three are out with other people. One hasn't started. Let's start it."

## Beat 1: find dates (40 seconds)
Open AIT. Stepper shows step 1. Press Find dates. The panel explains who was checked and who will be asked by email. Three options per quarter with reasons. Q3 warning: only two windows. "It checked five partner calendars, found the blocks, ranked them, and wrote the proposal. It also told her Q3 is tight instead of hiding it." Scroll the one-pager. Approve.

## Beat 2: partner sign-off (25 seconds)
Draft email to the partners. "Before the portco ever sees a date, the partners sign off. That's Peggy's rule." Approve and send. Waiting on the partners. Shift+P, then Partner replies. Five yeses land in the activity log.

## Beat 3: company picks (30 seconds)
Draft email to Tom Haggerty, the AIT CEO. Approve and send. Waiting on the portco. Shift+P, then Company picks. Quarter chips fill with the chosen dates.

## Beat 4: board confirms, with the conflict (60 seconds)
Draft email to the board. Approve and send. Shift+P, then Board conflict. Raymond Cho declines Q3. The agent proposes the number two Q3 window from the shortlist the partners already approved, re-checks the five partner calendars, and writes two sentences explaining what changed. "It didn't guess a new date. It went back to the list Peggy already approved and checked again." Approve the re-send. Shift+P, then Board confirms the re-send. Four quarters confirmed.

## Beat 5: lock and book (30 seconds)
Press Lock and book. Hotel and restaurant per meeting, near the office, one reason each. Approve and lock. Four lock icons. Work strip: Confirmed 8 of 20.

## Beat 6: the scale picture (25 seconds, Devrin)
Toggle to "All assistants." Eighteen rows. Open Calendar. 2027 fills with meetings. "Three assistants, fifteen companies. The portfolio is twenty-five. And when the next fund goes to market, the same engine schedules two hundred and fifty LP meetings."

## If time is short
Load `council-at-board` and start at Beat 4. The conflict is the wow moment. Never skip it.
